### 💫 About Me
I am an AI and Data Science student at **Vignan Institute of Technology and Science**, bridging the gap between predictive logic and immersive user experience. I specialize in crafting "glassmorphic" digital solutions, from IoT-based automation to high-performance 3D web environments.

- 🔭 Currently scaling **ProjectBuddy** (A mentor-guided academic ecosystem).
- 🎨 Crafting a **Cinematic Portfolio** using **Three.js** and **GSAP** for a 3D interactive experience.
- 🛡️ Architect of **LinkGuard AI**, an Android system for real-time malicious URL analysis.
- ⚡ Fun fact: I find inspiration in the determination of *Naruto* and hold an **NCC 'A' Certificate**.
